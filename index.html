Whiteboard Lab

Draw a concept from memory with your pencil, save it, reopen it later. Nothing leaves this browser: boards are kept in localStorage; PNG downloads a picture.

Pencil draws with pressure. Once the pencil has touched the board, fingers are ignored (palm rejection); tap Touch: off to allow finger drawing. Two-finger gestures still scroll/zoom the page outside the board.

What to try on the iPad
  1. Add this page to the Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) so it opens full screen.
  2. Draw a small diagram: boxes, arrows, labels. Does the line follow the pencil without lag? Does pressure change the width?
  3. Rest your palm while writing: does it leave marks?
  4. Save, close, reopen from the list below: same drawing?
  5. Select: drag a box around some strokes, drag them somewhere else, then Duplicate and Delete. Undo reverts each step.
